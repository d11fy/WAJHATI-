# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Small Arab merchants (Palestine first, then the wider Arab world) who want an online store fast, without technical skill. They sell physical goods, take orders over WhatsApp and bank/wallet transfer, and run the business from a phone. Their customers are Arabic-speaking shoppers on mobile browsing a store link shared on social media or WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

WAJHATI (واجهتي) is a multi-tenant store builder: a merchant signs up, gets a store on a link, picks one of eight storefront themes, adds products, and receives orders. The platform owner bills merchants through two plans (Starter and Pro) and confirms payments manually. Success is a merchant with a live, good-looking store taking real orders within minutes of signing up.

## Positioning

Arabic-first and right-to-left from the foundation (not a translated Western template); local payment reality (bank transfer, PalPay, Jawwal Pay, USDT, Vodafone Cash, WhatsApp hand-off); eight ready storefront themes; two clearly different plans; strict per-store data isolation, email verification and backups.

## Operating Context

Production at https://wajhati.store on the owner's own server. Public sign-up is open. Prices are in Israeli shekels (Starter 29/month or 199/year, Pro 39/month or 275/year). Payment is by transfer to a number and uploading the receipt, or WhatsApp contact. Expired trial or subscription pauses the public store (visitors see a "temporarily paused" page); the owner can still sign in and subscribe.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Stack: Next.js 16, Tailwind v4 with custom CSS, PostgreSQL, no UI component library installed (no shadcn, no motion library). Arabic default locale with English available. Starter: 30 products, basic themes, no luxury/premium themes; Pro: 200 products, enhanced, luxury and premium themes. Legal pages are approved and must stay reachable. No fabricated customers, testimonials, statistics or prices on any page.

## Brand Commitments

Name: واجهتي / WAJHATI. The owner has approved a full visual redesign of the platform identity; the name and the domain stay.

## Evidence on Hand

Real prices, plan limits, payment methods and legal text exist in the product. There are no real customer testimonials, case studies, merchant counts or sales figures: none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Arabic and RTL are the primary design case, checked first, then English.
2. A merchant must understand the offer and start a store within seconds.
3. Mobile first: both the merchant and the shopper are on phones.
4. Trust is shown through real facts (prices, payment methods, legal, backups), never invented proof.
5. Every storefront theme looks like a real shop, not a template demo.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Arabic readability (generous line height, no letter-spacing on Arabic), logical CSS properties, reduced-motion respected, WCAG AA contrast.
